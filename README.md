## 👋 About Me

I'm an undergraduate in India exploring **AI research, mathematics, C++ engineering, GPU computing, and computer graphics**. I enjoy building things from first principles, testing ideas through experiments, and understanding the algorithms behind the tools we use.

- ⚡ Building **MiniPyPy**, a CUDA/C++ deep learning framework with autograd and a tensor engine.
- 🧬 Developing **Morphos**, an artificial-life research project studying evolution and adaptation in simulated environments.
- 🩺 Exploring medical language processing through **MedCoder**.
- 📈 Building **Quant Assistant**, combining finance-literature RAG with a separate backtesting pipeline.
- 🎮 Learning **Vulkan**, rendering pipelines, and low-level graphics programming.

> Research. Build. Experiment. Measure. Repeat.

### ⚡ MiniPyPy — Deep Learning from Scratch

`C++` · `CUDA` · `pybind11` · `Autograd` · `GPU Kernels`

Building a PyTorch-inspired tensor framework with custom CUDA operations, reverse-mode differentiation, broadcasting, reductions, and performance-focused memory management. **TensorFold** explores higher-dimensional tensor algebra and decomposition.

### 🧬 Morphos — Artificial Evolution

`C++17` · `Artificial Life` · `Evolutionary Computation`

Investigating how simulated organisms adapt under environmental pressures. Experiments measure survival and robustness under different conditions; longer-term research explores evolved controllers and possible robotics applications.

### 🩺 MedCoder — Medical AI

`Python` · `NLP` · `LLMs`

Exploring clinical language understanding and research-driven medical coding workflows.

### 📈 Quant Assistant — Quantitative Research

`Python` · `RAG` · `Backtesting` · `Risk Management`

Combining a citation-grounded research assistant for quantitative finance literature with a separate, testable strategy and paper-trading pipeline.
